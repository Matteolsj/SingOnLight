SingOnLight - Story

The Grumpy Dragon

In a peaceful kingdom with fragile walls lives a dragon whose snoring is so powerful that it causes tremors

and makes paintings fall. To prevent disaster, the villagers invented an ancient ritual (a little improvised): whenever the sacred LED

lights up, someone must make a quick sound into a microphone to wake the dragon before it falls deeply asleep.

Too slow, and the dragon snores, bringing chaos; Too fast, and it grumbles because you woke it for nothing.

Only a perfectly timed response can preserve the peace… and the kingdom’s relative quiet.
